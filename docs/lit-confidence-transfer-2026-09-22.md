# Report: protocols for testing whether a KV-cache channel carries sender uncertainty

**Summary.** No latent-communication paper measures whether the receiver's uncertainty tracks the sender's; the closest (Confidence Laundering) uses AUROC against sender semantic-entropy labels. The knowledge-conflict and calibration literatures give ready-made construction rules and metrics; the main changes for us are (1) filter to examples the receiver gets right on clean text, (2) report probability mass on original vs. substituted answer plus JSD between conditions, not just Spearman, and (3) add "other-example" and moment-matched random controls with paired bootstrap CIs.

## Thread 1: uncertainty in latent / cache communication

**Confidence Laundering** (arXiv 2606.20662, Shi et al.). Position paper with one experiment: Qwen-1.5B on HotpotQA in a web-search tool loop. Sender uncertainty label = semantic entropy over K=20 sampled answers, binarised. Downstream component receives one of four handoffs (answer-only / answer+scalar score / answer+textual uncertainty description / answer+latent carrier from upstream hidden state) and is scored by AUROC at ranking high-SE examples; latent wins (numbers not extracted from HTML; unverified). Probes at blocks 7/14/28; deeper better. No ECE/Brier, no deranged control, N not stated. *Bearing: supports our question directly and gives the metric (AUROC vs sender-SE label); warns that they never controlled for content leakage.*

**Cache-to-Cache** (2510.03215, Fu et al.). Projector+fusion+gate between KV caches; accuracy only (OpenBookQA, MMLU-Redux, ARC-C, C-Eval), plus effective-rank of K/V. No entropy or calibration. *Bearing: nothing to copy on uncertainty; their progressive-replacement ablation (fraction of layers fused) is a useful dose-response control.*

**Latent Cache Flow** (2605.22863, Rossi et al.). Cross-context HotpotQA-bridge (n=5,899), Qwen3-0.6B pair; EM/F1 only; controls are question-only, half-context, full-context, and text-to-text at 50–200 token budgets. *Bearing: token-budget-matched text baseline is worth copying; no uncertainty.*

**Closed-form KV map** (2608.03893, Heo et al.). Ridge regression on 500 FineWeb-Edu sequences; HellaSwag accuracy retention 73–98%. No calibration. *Bearing: none on uncertainty.*

**Causal audit of relayed KV caches** (2608.04893, Cheng et al.) and **Do Latent Channels Actually Communicate?** (2607.26773). The first: pre-registered, five seeds, controls = deranged, zeroed, moment-matched random cache; Holm-corrected TOST equivalence tests; accuracy only. The second: controls = no message, current-example, *other-example (length-matched)*, self-generated (compute-matched); metrics include **Jensen–Shannon divergence at the first completion token** and teacher-forced continuations; paired bootstrap 95% CIs, ±1pp equivalence margin; N=40–100 per cell. Neither reports receiver calibration. *Bearing: changes our controls — deranged alone is weak; add other-example and moment-matched random. JSD at first answer token is the cleanest distribution-level metric for text-vs-cache.*

## Thread 2: constructing conflicting / unanswerable passages

**Longpre et al. 2021** (2109.05052, EMNLP). Entity substitution on NQ/NewsQA answer spans of type person/date/numeric/org/location: corpus substitution (same-type entity from the dataset), type-swap, popularity, alias. Evaluated **only on examples the model answered correctly before substitution**. Metric: memorisation ratio MR = p_o/(p_o+p_s), with a third "other" bucket. *Bearing: supports; gives the filter and the three-way outcome count.*

**Xie et al. 2023 Adaptive Chameleon** (2305.13300, ICLR 2024). Elicit parametric memory closed-book (answer + reason), build counter-answer by substitution, then have ChatGPT generate a fluent counter-evidence paragraph; filter with DeBERTa-v2 NLI entailment (99% manual agreement) and an answer-consistency check. Eval is multiple choice: memory answer / counter answer / **Uncertain**. Sizes 3,787–9,544 PopQA per model. Finding: coherent counter-evidence is followed far more than word-level substitution. *Bearing: warns that a crudely inserted sentence may be dismissed as incoherent; if we insert a contradiction, make it fluent and NLI-check that it entails the counter-answer.*

**ConflictBank** (2408.12076, NeurIPS 2024 D&B). Claims from Wikidata; evidence generated by LLaMA-3-70B in three styles; DeBERTa entailment filter; metrics OAR, CAR, MR=OAR/(OAR+CAR); "(D) uncertain" option; token-probability selection over options. *Bearing: same metric family; the explicit uncertain option is how they operationalise abstention.*

**Context-faithful prompting** (2303.11315, Zhou et al.). Knowledge-conflict set = NQ with Longpre-style substitution, restricted to examples the model gets right closed-book; abstention set = RealTime QA with an "I don't know" option, 113 items (63 answerable / 50 unanswerable); metrics MR, **Brier score on answerability**, HasAns/NoAns accuracy. *Bearing: gives the Brier-on-answerability protocol for our answer-removed variant.*

**SQuAD 2.0** (1806.03822). Crowd-written unanswerable questions; scoring treats no-answer as empty string, threshold tuned on dev, HasAns/NoAns splits. *Bearing: our "answer sentence removed" variant is a cheap unanswerable construction; report HasAns/NoAns separately.*

**Jin et al. Cutting Off the Head** (2402.18154). Template "The capital of X is {false}. Q: … A:"; metrics RM = f_m/(f_m+f_c+f_o) plus logit-lens probabilities of memory vs. context answer; 1,000 test / 200 dev. **Three Regimes** (2605.11574, 2026): assertive distractor sentences from Wikidata on 450 PopQA items stratified by popularity; GEE logistic regression with item-level clustering; log-odds effect sizes. *Bearing: the inserted-sentence design is standard; measure P(original) vs P(counter) at the answer token, and use item-clustered regression rather than pooled correlations.*

## Thread 3: reading uncertainty from hidden states / answer distributions

**Farquhar et al. 2024** (Nature, doi 10.1038/s41586-024-07421-0). 10 samples at T=1.0 (p=0.9, k=50) + one at T=0.1; clusters via bidirectional entailment (GPT-3.5 for sentences, DeBERTa-Large-MNLI for short claims); SE = −Σ P(C_i) log P(C_i) with length-normalised log-probs, or discrete count version. Correctness: SQuAD F1 ≥ 0.5 or GPT-4 judge. Metrics AUROC and AURAC; **400 test items per dataset** (TriviaQA, SQuAD, BioASQ, NQ-Open, SVAMP). SE AUROC 0.790 vs naive entropy 0.691, P(True) 0.698, embedding regression 0.687. *Bearing: gives sample size, correctness rule, and the baselines a reviewer will expect.*

**Semantic Entropy Probes** (2406.15927, Kossen et al.). Logistic probe on hidden states at TBG (last input token) or SLT; label = SE binarised at MSE-optimal threshold; 2,000 pairs short-form; AUROC vs SE and vs accuracy; SEPs generalise across datasets 7–10 AUROC points better than accuracy probes. *Bearing: TBG position (before generation) is exactly where our projected cache sits; the label choice (SE, not correctness) matches "does the channel carry uncertainty".*

**Orgad et al.** (2410.02707, ICLR 2025). Probe the **exact answer tokens**; AUC 0.85–0.95 in-task vs logit baselines 0.51–0.79; but probes fail to generalise across datasets. *Bearing: warns that a SQuAD-only result may be dataset-specific; readout should be at the answer token, not a generic last token.*

**Kadavath et al. 2022** (2207.05221). P(True) prompt; ECE with 10 equal-mass bins; Brier; AUROC; calibration charts; multiple-choice with lettered options is well calibrated. *Bearing: use 10 equal-mass bins for ECE and report Brier alongside.*

## Thread 4: receiver tracking a sender's epistemic state

**How Is Uncertainty Propagated in KD?** (2601.18909). Student–teacher comparison via predictive variance/entropy; finding: LLM students "systematically underestimate the teacher's predictive variance" (BioASQ, 10–20 students). *Bearing: warns that a learned projector will likely compress uncertainty; test variance ratio and calibration slope, not only rank correlation.*

**Evidential KD** (2507.18366). Dirichlet student matched to a Bayesian/prompt-ensemble teacher; validated by ECE, Brier, NLL, OOD AUROC, Wasserstein-1 between entropy distributions. *Bearing: Wasserstein-1 between text-condition and cache-condition entropy distributions is a compact summary we can add.*

**Speculative Verification** (2509.24328). Across 90 draft/companion/target triples: draft entropy alone poorly predicts target acceptance; information gain from draft–companion similarity is 4–13%, 30–40% jointly; draft–companion vs draft–target similarity correlates 0.75–0.87. *Bearing: warns that one model's confidence predicts another's only weakly by default; our null hypothesis should be "weak but positive".*

**CAUC cascades** (2609.11446) and **Uncertainty Propagation in LLM Systems** (2604.23505, survey). Cascades use temperature-scaled small-model confidence for deferral; the survey explicitly lists "measuring propagation fidelity across a boundary" as unsolved. *Bearing: supports novelty claim; no protocol to copy.*

## Thread 5: confidence in text vs. other carriers

**Tian et al. 2023** (2305.14975). Verbalised vs conditional-probability confidence; ECE, ECE-t, Brier, selective-accuracy AUC; 1,000 TriviaQA, 1,000 SciQ, 817 TruthfulQA; verbalised cuts ECE >50% on RLHF models. **Asking Is Not Enough** (2605.27752, 2026): the gap's sign flips with token readout (answer-span mean vs first token) and conditioning prefix; supplied plausible wrong answers get almost the same verbalised confidence as gold. *Bearing: warns that our "text confidence" must fix readout and prefix a priori; report both first-token and span-mean.*

**Yona et al. 2024** (2405.16908). Intrinsic confidence = 1 − fraction of k=20 samples contradicting the answer; decisiveness scored by judge; faithfulness = 1 − |decisiveness − confidence|; 932 PopQA + 932 NQ, ambiguous questions excluded. Models near 0.5 baseline. *Bearing: verbalised hedging is a weak carrier; supports the claim that a latent carrier could beat text, and gives the sampling-based sender-confidence definition.*

Confidence Laundering's four-way handoff (Thread 1) is the only direct text-vs-latent comparison found.

## What to change in our design

1. **Filter first**: keep only SQuAD items where the receiver answers the clean *text* correctly (EM, or F1 ≥ 0.5 per Farquhar) and, separately, where the sender does; report N after filtering. Target ≥ 400 surviving items per variant (Farquhar's cell size), ideally 1,000.
2. **Contradiction construction**: use Longpre corpus substitution (same-type entity from SQuAD) for the counter-answer, then insert one fluent sentence asserting it; run DeBERTa-MNLI to confirm the sentence entails the counter-answer and does not entail the original (Xie/ConflictBank filter).
3. **Primary metrics per condition**: P(original answer), P(substituted answer), P(other) at the first answer token under teacher forcing, plus answer-token entropy; derive MR = p_o/(p_o+p_s). Also report span-mean log-prob (Asking Is Not Enough shows readout changes conclusions).
4. **Distribution-level metric**: JSD between the text-condition and cache-condition first-token distributions per item (2607.26773); Wasserstein-1 between the two entropy distributions across items (2507.18366).
5. **Sender-tracking test**: replace bare Spearman with AUROC of receiver-cache entropy predicting a binarised sender label — sender semantic entropy from 10 samples at T=1 clustered by DeBERTa (Farquhar/SEP), thresholded at MSE-optimal split — and keep Spearman as secondary. Add ECE (10 equal-mass bins) and Brier of cache-condition confidence vs receiver correctness.
6. **Controls**: deranged cache, moment-matched random cache, zeroed cache (2608.04893), and *other-example length-matched* cache (2607.26773); plus text handoff with the sender's verbalised confidence (Confidence Laundering's "textual uncertainty" arm).
7. **Statistics**: pair everything at the item level; paired bootstrap 95% CIs; GEE or mixed logistic regression with item clustering for variant effects (Three Regimes); pre-register a TOST margin for "cache ≈ text".
8. **Generalisation check**: repeat on one non-SQuAD context-QA set (NQ or HotpotQA-distractor) — probes and error detectors are known not to transfer across datasets (Orgad).

Unverified: Confidence Laundering's exact AUROC numbers and N; Latent Cache Flow details beyond the HTML abstract/tables; Three Regimes is single-author and not peer-reviewed.
