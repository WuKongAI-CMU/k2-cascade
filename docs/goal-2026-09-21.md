# Long-term goal (set 2026-09-21, revisit 2026-12-21)

**Goal.** Show that frozen language models of different sizes (later: different families) can work together
through learned state channels — passing KV cache, not text — and that the channel carries what text cannot:
how sure the sender was. The end state is a small model that knows when it will fail and hands its *state*,
not a transcript, to a larger one.

**Why this and not something else.** Text is the only channel agents have today. It is lossy, slow, and
drops the sender's uncertainty. We have the first cross-size channel working (71% binding transfer, 3.7B→7B,
2026-09-21). Nobody has shown a cross-size channel carrying confidence. It fits one GPU at a time and a few
hundred dollars a month.

**Milestones with kill criteria.**
1. *Channel exists* (by 2026-10-12, CoRL EEAI workshop, 4 pages). Deranged-sender control shows most of the
   gain is content, not a learned prompt. Kill: if the control's retention ≥ 80% of the trained projector's,
   the "channel" is a soft prompt and the story changes to that.
2. *Channel carries real work* (by 2027-01, ICML/COLM). Passage QA instead of synthetic facts; message
   compression (how few layers/heads/bytes); reverse direction; one cross-family pair. Kill: if passage QA
   transfer < 30% of the text ceiling after tuning, stop scaling and publish the negative.
3. *Channel carries confidence* (by 2027-03). Sender reads ambiguous or contradictory facts; does the
   receiver's calibration track the sender's? Compare against text handoff with a verbal confidence.
   This is the milestone that makes the line worth more than an efficiency trick.
4. *Handoff policy* (by 2027-06). Pre-action failure probe (0.876 AUC on 3.7B) decides when to hand state
   to the 7B; measure accuracy per dollar against always-small, always-big, and text handoff.

**Rules.** One line at a time; new directions go into `docs/` as notes, not into the job queue. Every result
gets its control before its headline. Money: quote before spending beyond the standing $500 GCP allowance.
