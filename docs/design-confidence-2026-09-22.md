# Milestone 3 design: does the channel carry how sure the sender was? (2026-09-22, not yet run)

**Question.** When the sender's passage is ambiguous or contradictory, does the receiver reading the sender's
cache become *appropriately* less sure — the way it would if it had read the text itself — or does the
projected cache flatten everything into a confident answer? Text handoff with a verbal "I think" is the
comparison. This is the milestone that separates "a channel" from "an efficiency trick" (goal file, item 3).

**Materials.** Start from SQuAD passages (the QA test already works at F1 53 vs 55 text). Three passage
conditions per question, same question, same gold:
1. *clean* — original passage.
2. *contradicted* — a second sentence inserted that asserts a different answer to the same question
   (generated by the 375B judge from the gold answer; checked to be a plausible alternative, not gibberish).
3. *removed* — the sentence containing the gold span deleted (the answer is no longer in the passage).

**Receiver arms.** text (reads passage itself) · project (mapped cache) · derange (wrong passage's cache) ·
none. For each arm and condition we record the receiver's probability mass on the gold answer's first token
and on the contradicting answer's first token, and the entropy of the answer distribution.

**Measurements.**
- *Calibration tracking*: Spearman correlation across examples between the receiver's answer confidence under
  text and under project. If the channel carries confidence, the receiver's confidence when reading the
  cache should track its confidence when reading the text, per example — not just on average.
- *Condition sensitivity*: mean confidence and entropy in clean vs contradicted vs removed, under text and
  under project. Text is expected to drop in contradicted and removed; the question is whether project drops
  by the same amount. A channel that keeps clean-level confidence on removed passages is laundering.
- *Where the two answers go*: in contradicted, mass on gold vs mass on the alternative, text vs project.
- *Sender-side probe*: the 3.7B's own confidence (its answer distribution after reading the passage). Does
  the receiver under project track the sender's confidence or its own text-reading confidence? The
  interesting outcome is "the sender's" — the cache carries the sender's epistemic state, not the passage.

**Controls.** derange gives the floor for every measurement. The deranged-sender projector (soft prompt)
is the second control: its confidence pattern should not vary with the condition at all.

**Kill / proceed.** Proceed to a paper claim if (a) Spearman(text, project) ≥ 0.5 on clean and (b) project's
confidence drops on removed passages by ≥ 50% of text's drop. If (b) fails while F1 stays high, the finding
is "content transfers, uncertainty does not", which is publishable as a caution and points at training the
projector with a calibration term.

**Cost.** Eval-only on existing checkpoints (seed 2, control, ridge). One A100 for ~2 h including the 375B
generation of contradictions for 300 questions (API cost small). Under $10.

**Code needed.** `qa.py` gets `--variant clean|contradicted|removed` reading a jsonl with an extra field;
a small script builds the variants with the judge; a `confidence.py` that reports the four measurements.
About one working session.
